My design system

What stays the same from product to product. Each case study has its own color, type and icon details.

Transport#0494F9Feedback#259A1AAccess Map#CE3E04Housing Map#0475C8Zoning#760993
01

One brand color per product

A single saturated primary on light, neutral surfaces keeps the main action obvious.

02

Familiar navigation

A bottom tab bar with four labelled items on mobile, and modal panels for detail on web.

03

Rounded, touch-friendly parts

Pill buttons, rounded cards, chips and search fields that are easy to hit and easy to scan.

04

One flow skeleton

Every product starts from the same path: quick onboarding, sign up, then straight to the main task.
