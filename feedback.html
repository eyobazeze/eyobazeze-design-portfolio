Case study · Mobile app

Urban Feedback & Complaint Platform

A digital bridge between citizens and city administration. A citizen-to-government interface that simplifies reporting, ensures accountability and builds trust through transparency.

RoleUI/UX design
PlatformMobile
FocusCitizen reporting
ToolsFigma, FigJam

The brief

01

The problem

  • Existing reporting methods are fragmented or too slow.
  • Citizens don't know whether complaints are received or processed.
  • How can citizens report issues easily while officials get usable data and the process stays transparent?
02

Design goals

  • Quick, intuitive reporting with photos, text and precise geolocation.
  • Real-time tracking of complaint status.
  • A transparent public dashboard of common issues and their frequency.
  • Management tools for staff to categorise, prioritise and close cases.
03

Impact

  • Citizens: more confidence that their voices are heard, leading to stronger engagement.
  • Administrators: structured data and analytics to prioritise interventions.
  • The city: transparency and accountability that build trust.

Target users

Citizens

Residents of all ages who want an accessible way to report issues, from young professionals to elderly community members.

City administrators

Municipal staff who log, track and resolve reported issues.

Community groups and NGOs

Organisations that monitor city services and advocate for improvements.

Research and personas

I began by analysing global civic engagement tools such as SeeClickFix and FixMyStreet, studying their strengths and shortcomings. Local insights from Ethiopian municipalities highlighted a lack of digital infrastructure for citizen engagement, which shaped the focus on accessibility and simplicity.

Iconography

Outline icons in the tab bar, each with a label. Category chips pair a small green glyph with a text label (Roads, Lighting, Waste, Transport). The active tab turns green.

UX principles

Usability principles behind the screens, and where they show up.

01

Visibility of system status

My Report lists every submission with its status, so citizens can see whether a complaint has been received and processed.

02

Minimal effort

The report form asks for a photo, category, short description and a confirmed location, nothing more.

03

User control

A preview screen with Post and Draft lets people check a report before it goes out.

04

Transparency

A public feed with category filters shows what others have reported and how often.

Reflection and what's next

Future improvements

  • AI-based categorisation of issues using image recognition.
  • Integration with government resource management systems for faster resolution.
  • Expanded offline functionality for users with limited connectivity.
